## Frequently asked questions

#### Which company is leading in robotics?

According to Yahoo, the biggest robotic companies in the field include the following:

* Honda Motor
* Siemens AG
* Sony
* Denso Corp
* Midea Group

Other well-known companies include Boston Dynamics, Zebra Technologies, Seiko Epson, and KION Group.

#### What are the different types of robotics companies?

But, because the robotics field is so complex and nuanced, many companies focus on one smaller robotics element, and they create top-quality components and solutions.

* Artificial intelligence products
* Autonomous robots like the Roomba
* Collaborative robots
* Computer vision robots
* Consumer robots
* Factory automation tools and 3D printing robots
* Humanoid robots
* Industrial robots
* Intuitive surgical robots
* Manufacturing robots for the automotive and aerospace industries
* Mobile robots
* Motion control products
* Robotic control systems
* Semiconductor automation solutions

Robotic companies may also specialize in working with certain industries and businesses, like the manufacturing industry, software development companies, healthcare businesses, and the industrial automation field.

#### What is the difference between a robot company and a robotics company?

There’s a slight difference between a robot company and a robotics company. The term “robot company” refers to the actual robots built. An example of a robot company would be iRobot, the company which manufactures the Roomba.

In contrast, “robotics company” refers to a company that builds the systems and technology behind a robot, not just the robots. Nachi Robotic Systems, Inc. is a company that produces robots, robotic technology, and more.

#### Is robotics a good career?

Robotics can be a highly rewarding career. It’s a challenging field, but it’s also constantly evolving. As a robotics engineer, you’ll face daily obstacles, but you’ll also get to put your skills and knowledge to use to help overcome those obstacles. If you want to work for a company, you’ll have a vast selection of potential employers, but you can also explore the option of founding your own company.

Depending on your specialty, you might have the satisfaction of making vehicles safer, advancing the newest surgical techniques to help save lives, or developing robots that automate processes and enhance the quality of life for the general public.

#### What is the salary of a robotics engineer?

Recruiter.com reports that robotics engineer salaries range from $55,360 to $166,050. Those salaries vary depending on your experience, specialty, education, and even location.

If you want full control over your salary and career path, consider offering robotics engineer services as an independent contractor, or think about opening your own robotics company.
