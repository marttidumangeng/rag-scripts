Summary: Robotics companies are building AI-powered systems for manufacturing, logistics, healthcare, defense and agriculture. From humanoid warehouse bots to surgical assistants and delivery vehicles, innovators like Amazon Robotics, FANUC and Tesla are transforming how tasks are automated across industries.

[Robots](https://builtin.com/robotics) are taking over the world. OK, not really. [Not yet](https://builtin.com/robotics/future-robots-robotics) . But they are becoming increasingly prevalent in almost every industry, from [healthcare](https://builtin.com/robotics/surgical-medical-healthcare-robotics-companies) and manufacturing to defense and [education](https://builtin.com/robotics/robotics-in-the-classroom) .

At [robotics companies](https://builtin.com/robotics/publicly-traded-robotics-companies) across America, the co-mingling of engineering and science is producing some truly innovative products — things that do what humans have typically done, only better. Whether it’s welding, teaching, assembling cars or performing surgery, these inventions are [changing the way we live and work](https://builtin.com/robotics/robo-banking-robotics-applications-banking-and-finance) .

The following companies are contributing to the [robotics revolution](https://builtin.com/robotics/future-robots-robotics) .

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are the big 4 in robotics? (companies)

The big 4 in robotics include ABB, FANUC, KUKA and Yaskawa. These are some of the largest robotics companies in the world and are recognized for their advancements in industrial robotics.

### What is the difference between industrial and service robotics companies

The main difference between industrial and service robotics companies is who they develop their system for.

Industrial Robotic Companies: develop automation systems to improve manufacturing efficiency. Their robotic systems include welding, assembly and painting robots.

Service Robotics Companies: create humanoid and nonhumanoid robots that assist people in industries like healthcare, agriculture and home use.

### How is AI used by robotics companies?

AI is integral to many robotic systems as a whole and in several functions as well. Robotics companies are using AI and machine learning for features like perceptions and sensing, decision-making and autonomous navigation. By incorporating AI, many developers aim to make their systems smarter and adaptive to the real world.

### Where are most robotics companies located?

Robotics companies are located in various cities and countries; however there are several hubs that have strong tech workforces and backing for robotics companies. They include cities like Silicon Valley and Cambridge, and countries such as Germany and Japan.
