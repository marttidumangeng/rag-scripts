Rezilient is building the world’s first distributed healthcare system. A network of CloudClinics allow members to see their physicians at any location, regardless of where that doctor is located, because the doctors’ skills can be transported over the internet to any CloudClinic, anywhere in the country, instantly. Each CloudClinic is staffed by a nurse who facilitates the high-touch aspects of the appointment, like taking the patient’s vitals, labs, and physical exams. The doctor appears on a large monitor, while the nurse acts as the doctor’s hands.What makes these CloudClinics especially unique is Rezilient’s cloud health platform. It enables the doctor to, for example, listen to your heart or examine your eardrum remotely with integrated digital medical devices, and on the large screen the patient gets to see what the doctor sees in real time.This means Rezilient members can see the same physician and expect the same patient experience in a CloudClinic in Miami as they would in Phoenix.
